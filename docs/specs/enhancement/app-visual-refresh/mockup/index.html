The three FAIL rows are pre-existing defects on main, not regressions introduced here — #C9821B has never met AA as small text. They surface now because this change re-derives every contrast pair. jest-axe under jsdom cannot evaluate color-contrast (no layout engine), which is why QA-11 has been passing while these ship (Kaizen KZ-002).

Card-vs-canvas separation of 1.05:1 is deliberately subtle — the depth comes from the recalibrated shadow ladder and warm borders, not from darkening the page. A deeper canvas alternative (#F7F3ED, separation 1.11:1) is recorded as OQ-2 in the proposal if this reads too soft on a real screen.

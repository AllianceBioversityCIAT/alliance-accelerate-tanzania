**Curated, not complete — and the 30 discarded frames are gone, not archived.** 41 PNGs were produced; 11 are kept, the ones `execution.md` cites as evidence for a requirement. The rest (intermediate drag frames, and 12 frames of observation (v)'s check, which was declared *not attempted* rather than resolved) were **discarded because they evidence no requirement — not because they can be retrieved.** They were never committed (`git log --diff-filter=A -- captures/` was empty at the time of curation), so they are unrecoverable. ⚠️ An earlier draft of this file justified the curation with *"git history is permanent"*. That is true only of files committed at least once, which these never were — a false warrant that would have told an archive reader a re-run was unnecessary. Caught by the T-7 reviewer and settled by measurement. The driver scripts were not committed either — `design.md` §13 proposes the committed harness this spec deliberately did not build.

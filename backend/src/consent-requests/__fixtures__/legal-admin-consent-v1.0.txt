ACCELERATE Tanzania Registry Consent for Publication of Information (Administrator-Managed Registration)
The ACCELERATE Tanzania Registry is an online platform operated by the International Center for Tropical Agriculture (CIAT), through its Tanzania office, to increase the visibility of actors involved in seed systems and agricultural value chains, facilitate business and professional connections, and support access to market opportunities and services.
For purposes of the ACCELERATE Tanzania Registry, CIAT acts as the organization responsible for the collection, storage, use, and publication of information submitted through the Registry.
Before providing your consent, please read this document carefully.
	•	Purpose of the Registry
The Registry is intended to facilitate connections among seed companies, traders, processors, NGOs, humanitarian organizations, digital service providers, research institutions, and other actors operating within agricultural value chains.
The information published through the Registry is intended to allow users to identify relevant actors, establish business relationships, access services, and connect with organizations operating in specific crops, value chains, and geographic areas.
	•	Information that may be Collected and Published
The information collected and published through the Registry may include:
	•	Organization or partner name;
	•	Partner type;
	•	Contact person name;
	•	Position or role;
	•	Sex;
	•	Region and district;
	•	Main and other crops;
	•	Annual average capacity in tonnes;
	•	Telephone number;
	•	Email address;
	•	GPS coordinates and other location information; and
	•	Any other information voluntarily provided for inclusion in the Registry.
I understand that any information provided and authorized for publication may be displayed through the Registry and made available to users worldwide.
	•	Public Availability of Information
I understand and acknowledge that:
	•	The Registry is publicly accessible through the internet.
	•	Information published through the Registry may be viewed by any person worldwide without registration or login requirements.
	•	Information published through the Registry may be copied, shared, downloaded, referenced, indexed by internet search engines, or otherwise accessed by third parties.
	•	Once information has been accessed, copied, downloaded, or indexed by third parties, CIAT may not be able to prevent further use of such information by those third parties.
	•	Business Information and Location Information
I understand that some of the information submitted may be commercially sensitive or may reveal details regarding the activities, operations, capacity, or location of an organization.
In particular, I understand that:
	•	Annual average capacity in tonnes may reveal information regarding the scale or capacity of an organization's operations.
	•	Telephone numbers and email addresses may enable direct contact by third parties.
	•	GPS coordinates may reveal the precise location of facilities, operations, offices, activities, or other physical locations associated with the organization.
I acknowledge these implications and voluntarily authorize publication of such information through the Registry.
	•	International Storage and Access
I understand and expressly agree that:
	•	Information submitted through the Registry may be stored, processed, and maintained outside Tanzania.
	•	The Registry is hosted on cloud infrastructure located in Ireland.
	•	Information published through the Registry may be accessed by individuals and organizations located both within and outside Tanzania.
	•	The information provided may therefore be subject to international transfer, storage, processing, and access.
I expressly consent to such international transfer, storage, processing, and public availability of the information provided for inclusion in the Registry.

	•	Authority to Provide Information
By signing this Consent, I confirm that:
	•	I am authorized to provide information on behalf of the organization identified below.
	•	The information provided is accurate to the best of my knowledge.
	•	I have the authority to authorize publication of the information through the Registry.
	•	Where I provide personal information relating to another individual, I am authorized to provide such information and to authorize its publication through the Registry.
	•	I understand that CIAT may request reasonable evidence supporting the representations and authorizations made in this Consent, including evidence of my authority to act on behalf of the organization and, where applicable, to provide and authorize publication of personal information relating to another individual.
	•	I agree to provide such supporting documentation or evidence upon request by CIAT.
	•	Where I provide information relating to another individual, I acknowledge that I am responsible for ensuring that I have the necessary authority and permissions to provide such information for publication through the Registry.
	•	Rights of Data Subjects
You have the right, at any time, to:
	•	Request access to the information associated with your profile.
	•	Request correction of inaccurate, incomplete, or outdated information.
	•	Request updates to your profile.
	•	Request removal of your profile and associated information from the Registry.
	•	Withdraw your consent to the publication and use of your information.
	•	Request information regarding how your data is being used, stored, or disclosed through the Registry.
	•	Submit a complaint in accordance with applicable law.
Withdrawal of consent will not affect any processing already carried out before the withdrawal request is received.

	•	The worldwide accessibility of such information through the internet.
